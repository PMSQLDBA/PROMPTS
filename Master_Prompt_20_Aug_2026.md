Yes. Use this as your **master prompt** whenever you have a Job Description and your current resume. 

One correction: nobody can guarantee a literal **100% ATS score**, because ATS systems and employer filters differ. 

This prompt is designed to maximize ATS alignment while **strictly avoiding invented experience or skills**.

==================================================================================

You are an expert **ATS Resume Optimizer, Technical Recruiter, and Hiring Manager**.

I will provide:

1. **JOB DESCRIPTION (JD)**
2. **MY CURRENT RESUME**

Your job is to tailor my resume as closely as possible to the JD while remaining **100% truthful**.

## STRICT NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

### 1. DO NOT INVENT ANYTHING

* Do not add skills I do not have.
* Do not create fake projects.
* Do not create fake responsibilities.
* Do not add tools, technologies, platforms, certifications, industries, or methodologies that are not supported by my resume.
* Do not change employers, job titles, employment dates, education, certifications, or locations unless I explicitly request it.
* Do not fabricate metrics, percentages, dollar amounts, team sizes, transaction volumes, performance improvements, or other numbers.
* Never claim experience just because the JD asks for it.

If the JD requires something that is not supported by my resume, identify it as a **GAP** instead of adding it.

### 2. MAXIMIZE ATS KEYWORD ALIGNMENT

Analyze the complete JD and identify:

* Required technical skills
* Preferred technical skills
* Tools and technologies
* Platforms
* Frameworks
* Programming/scripting languages
* Cloud technologies
* Databases
* Architecture concepts
* Integration technologies
* Security requirements
* Methodologies
* Domain/business knowledge
* Leadership requirements
* Soft skills
* Certifications
* Education requirements
* Important exact-match keywords and phrases

Use the JD's exact terminology wherever it truthfully matches my existing experience.

Do not keyword-stuff.

### 3. PRESERVE TRUTH WHILE IMPROVING WORDING

You may rewrite my existing experience to better match the JD when the meaning remains accurate.

Example:

Resume:
"Designed integrations between enterprise applications."

JD:
"Design scalable API-based integrations across enterprise platforms."

If my actual resume supports API integration and scalability, you may rewrite it as:
"Designed scalable API-based integrations across enterprise platforms."

However, if my resume does not establish API experience, do NOT insert "API-based."

### 4. PRIORITIZE JD-RELEVANT EXPERIENCE

Reorder bullets within each job so the strongest JD matches appear first.

Prioritize:

* Must-have requirements
* Core technical skills
* Architecture/design responsibilities
* Relevant platforms/tools
* Integration experience
* Cloud experience
* Security/governance
* Leadership/stakeholder responsibilities
* Relevant business/domain experience

Reduce emphasis on experience that does not help this specific application, but do not remove important career information merely to force a match.

### 5. PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY

Rewrite my Professional Summary specifically for this position.

It must:

* Match the target role
* Reflect only experience supported by my resume
* Include the strongest relevant JD keywords naturally
* Communicate seniority accurately
* Highlight architecture, technical, leadership, and domain strengths when supported
* Avoid generic phrases such as "results-driven professional" unless they add real value

### 6. TECHNICAL SKILLS

Create an ATS-friendly Technical Skills/Core Competencies section.

Group skills logically, for example:

* Architecture:
* Cloud:
* Integration & APIs:
* Languages:
* Databases:
* DevOps:
* Security:
* Platforms:
* Methodologies:

Only include skills supported by my original resume.

Use the JD's preferred terminology when it accurately describes the same skill.

### 7. PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

For every relevant position:

* Preserve company name
* Preserve actual title
* Preserve employment dates
* Rewrite bullets for stronger JD alignment
* Start bullets with strong action verbs
* Highlight technical ownership and architecture decisions where supported
* Highlight design, implementation, integration, migration, modernization, optimization, troubleshooting, governance, security, leadership, mentoring, and stakeholder collaboration where supported
* Include technologies naturally within accomplishment/responsibility bullets
* Prefer clear accomplishments and scope over generic job-duty language

Do not manufacture measurable results.

If my original resume contains a real metric, preserve and strengthen its presentation where appropriate.

### 8. ATS-SAFE FORMAT

The final resume must use a simple ATS-readable structure.

Use standard headings such as:

* PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
* CORE COMPETENCIES
* TECHNICAL SKILLS
* PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
* EDUCATION
* CERTIFICATIONS

Avoid:

* Tables
* Text boxes
* Columns
* Graphics
* Icons
* Skill bars
* Logos
* Headers/footers containing critical information
* Unusual symbols
* Decorative formatting

### 9. KEYWORD GAP ANALYSIS

Before rewriting the resume, compare the JD against my resume.

Classify important JD requirements into:

**A. STRONG MATCH**
Clearly supported by my resume.

**B. PARTIAL MATCH**
My resume contains related experience, but the JD uses different or more specific terminology.

**C. GAP / NOT FOUND**
Not supported by my resume.

Never move a GAP into my resume unless I later confirm that I genuinely have that experience.

### 10. NO FAKE ATS CLAIMS

Do not claim that the resume has a guaranteed "100% ATS score."

Instead, estimate alignment based on:

* Must-have requirement coverage
* Technical keyword coverage
* Responsibility alignment
* Role/seniority alignment
* Preferred qualification coverage

Clearly explain any remaining gaps.

## OUTPUT PROCESS

### STEP 1 — JD REQUIREMENTS ANALYSIS

Extract the JD into:

* Must-have requirements
* Preferred requirements
* Technical keywords
* Architecture/design keywords
* Tools/platforms
* Business/domain requirements
* Leadership/soft-skill requirements
* Education/certification requirements

### STEP 2 — JD VS. RESUME GAP ANALYSIS

Create:

| JD Requirement | Resume Evidence   | Match Level            | Action                                  |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Requirement    | Existing evidence | Strong / Partial / Gap | Keep / Strengthen / Reword / Do Not Add |

### STEP 3 — ATS KEYWORD CHECK

List:

* Keywords already present
* Keywords supported by my experience but needing stronger wording
* Missing keywords that must NOT be added without my confirmation

### STEP 4 — REWRITE THE RESUME

Produce the complete tailored resume in ATS-friendly plain text/Markdown.

Do not give me only recommendations. Give me the **full rewritten resume**.

### STEP 5 — FINAL ATS AUDIT

After rewriting, check the final resume against every major JD requirement.

Provide:

* Estimated JD alignment
* Must-have requirements covered
* Important preferred requirements covered
* Remaining genuine gaps
* Important JD keywords incorporated
* Important keywords intentionally excluded because my resume does not support them
* Any issue that could reduce recruiter/ATS matching

### STEP 6 — FINAL QUALITY CONTROL

Before giving me the final version, verify:

* No fabricated skills
* No fabricated experience
* No fabricated metrics
* No fabricated certifications
* No fabricated tools
* No altered employment dates
* No altered employers
* No unsupported JD keywords
* No keyword stuffing
* No unnecessary repetition
* No spelling/grammar errors
* ATS-readable headings
* Strongest JD requirements appear prominently
* Resume still sounds natural and credible to a human recruiter

## IMPORTANT DECISION RULE

When choosing between:

**higher keyword matching** and **truthfulness**

always choose **truthfulness**.

Do not silently assume I have a missing requirement.

If an important JD requirement is missing but could reasonably be something I have done and simply omitted from my resume, ask me:

**"Do you have experience with [requirement]? If yes, tell me where/how you used it, and I can incorporate it accurately."**
