## 11. Hidden Achievement Discovery Prompt

Many engineers underestimate their daily contributions. Use this to uncover hidden value.

```text
Act as an Engineering Manager reviewing my last 12 months of work.

I will provide my daily activities, tickets, projects, meetings, and tasks.

Analyze them and identify hidden achievements.

Look for:
- Problems prevented
- Risks eliminated
- Manual work reduced
- Knowledge created
- Systems improved
- Processes standardized
- Customer/business impact
- Technical decisions influenced

Convert ordinary tasks into high-impact achievements.

For each achievement provide:

Achievement Title:
Problem:
My Contribution:
Technical Solution:
Impact:
Metrics I should track:
Business Value:
```

## A powerful habit:

Every Friday ask yourself:

```text
What did I improve this week?

What risk did I remove?

What manual work did I eliminate?

What system became better because of me?

How can I prove it with numbers?
```

For a Senior SQL Server DBA, your strongest promotion evidence usually comes from **automation + reliability + security + performance + business impact**, not the number of tickets closed.
